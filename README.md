# AyurCare — Authentic Ayurvedic Wellness Products

**Live site:** [https://ayurcareshop.vercel.app](https://ayurcareshop.vercel.app)

AyurCare is an e-commerce website for authentic Ayurvedic and herbal wellness products, based in Colombo, Sri Lanka. Its tagline — *"Nature's Wisdom Delivered to Your Door"* — captures the mission: bridging ancient Ayurvedic knowledge and modern online shopping convenience, with products "sourced for purity and backed by tradition."

## Overview

AyurCare was founded with a passion for traditional wellness. The company works directly with trusted, certified manufacturers to ensure every product meets strict quality standards, and is dedicated to bringing authentic Ayurvedic and herbal wellness products to people who seek natural, traditional remedies.

Core commitments:

- **100% authentic Ayurvedic products** sourced from certified manufacturers
- **All-natural ingredients** with no synthetic fillers
- **Every batch tested** for purity and potency
- **Fast, tracked delivery** across the island (Sri Lanka)
- **Expert customer support** and wellness guidance

## Key Features

- Online storefront with a catalog of **32 products** across four categories
- **10% discount** on all online orders
- Product **category and availability filters** (In Stock / Pre-order)
- **Sorting options**: Featured, Price (low → high / high → low), Highest Rated, Newest
- **Customer ratings and reviews** displayed per product
- Shopping cart and customer account ("Sign in" / "My Account")
- Contact form for customer inquiries and wellness guidance

## Site Structure

| Page | Route | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | `/` | Hero banner, featured products, highlights of offers and guarantees |
| Shop | `/shop` | Full product catalog ("All Products") with category/availability filters, sorting, ratings, and pagination |
| Categories | `/categories` | "Shop by Category" hub linking to the four product categories |
| About | `/about` | Company story, mission, and values |
| Contact | `/contact` | Contact form (Full Name, Email, Phone, Subject, Message) and contact details |
| Sign in | — | Customer account login |

The footer provides three sections on every page: **Customer Service** (Contact Us, About Us, Categories), **Quick Links** (Shop All, Shopping Cart, My Account), and **Get In Touch** (address, email, phone).
